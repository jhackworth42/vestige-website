Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Vestige is an editorial guide to Roman places. You can browse the app without creating an account.

What the app uses

The app includes its place catalog and photographs on the device. It does not ask for your name, account credentials, payment details, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or current location. It has no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting service added by Vestige.

Maps and location

Vestige shows the known coordinates of historic places on an Apple map. It does not request permission to access your current location or show your current position. The map component's Apple privacy declaration lists precise location for app functionality, without linking it to you or using it for tracking. Vestige itself does not turn on your current location or receive it. Apple Maps may process the network request needed to load map tiles under Apple's own privacy terms.

If you choose Directions, iOS opens Apple Maps with the selected place name and coordinates. Vestige does not receive your route or destination history.

External links and sharing

Opening a source, image credit, license, or support link leaves Vestige for the selected website or mail app. Those services may receive ordinary network or message information under their own policies. Sharing a place uses the iOS share sheet; Vestige does not learn which destination you choose.

Support email

If you email Vestige, we receive the address and information you choose to send. We use it to answer your request and keep the correspondence as needed to resolve the issue or document a factual or image-rights correction. You may ask us to delete correspondence at jop.hackworth@gmail.com, subject to any legal obligation to retain it.

Website

This site uses no analytics, advertising cookies, accounts, or contact form. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process standard connection information, such as your IP address and browser details, to deliver it. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its practices.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if the app adds accounts, analytics, advertising, location access, purchases, or other data use. For a privacy question, contact jop.hackworth@gmail.com.
