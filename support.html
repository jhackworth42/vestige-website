Support

Help with the app, a historic place, or an image is one email away.

Email jop.hackworth@gmail.com for support and feedback.

Report a problem

Tell us what happened, the screen or place affected, your iPhone model and iOS version, and the Vestige version. A screenshot helps when one is available.

Suggest a landmark correction

Include the place name, the wording or map location you think needs correction, and a reliable source if you have one.

Maps and photos

The place catalog and photos are included in the app. Apple Maps needs a network connection to show its map. If a photo or map pin is wrong, include the place name in your email.

General feedback

Ideas about reading, browsing, or collections are welcome at the same email address. Please do not send passwords or payment information.
